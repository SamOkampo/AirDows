Common questions

Does AirDows reduce quality?

No. It transfers the selected file without re-encoding or reducing its resolution.

Does it work across different networks?

Yes. AirDows attempts a direct route first and uses an encrypted TURN relay when network conditions require it.

Why can locking the iPhone interrupt it?

iOS can suspend Safari in the background. Keep the screen active during large transfers.
